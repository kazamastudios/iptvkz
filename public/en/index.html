Questions.

Is it really free? What's the catch?

There isn't one. IPTVKZ has no subscription, no ads, no sign-up and no paid version. The code is open too, for anyone who wants to check.

Does the app come with channels or lists?

No. IPTVKZ is just a player. You use the lists you add yourself and are responsible for what you add and watch. Only use content you have the right to watch. See the Terms of use.

What language is the app in?

The app interface is in Brazilian Portuguese for now. Lists in any language work, and audio and subtitle tracks come from what your list provides.

Do I need to keep a computer on?

Not with the LG, Android TV and Android apps, because everything runs on the device itself. The computer only needs to stay on if you watch in the browser of another device.

How do I update?

On LG and Android TV, the app checks for the latest version when it opens and updates itself. When the native part changes, Android offers the new APK and, on LG, you need to install the new .ipk. On the computer, download the new version from this page.

Where can I get help?

Tap Help, in the corner of the screen, for the most common answers. If that doesn't solve it, contact the team on the support page or write to suporte@kazamastudios.com.br. Don't send links, usernames or passwords from your lists.

IPTVKZ is only a media player. It does not provide, host, sell or recommend content, lists or subscriptions. Kazama Studios and the developers are not responsible for piracy, fraud or any misuse of the lists added by users.

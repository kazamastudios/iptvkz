Privacy policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026 · Controller: Kazama Studios

In short

  • The app does not send data to us, and we do not sell personal data.
  • Your lists, passwords, favorites and history stay only on your device.
  • No ads, no account, no usage analytics and no automatic error reports.
  • The website support form is optional, and what you write in it becomes public on GitHub.

This page is a translation. If there is any difference, the Portuguese version prevails.

1. Data the app stores

The lists you add (addresses, Xtream Codes username and password), the program guide, favorites, playback history, preferences and the parental control PIN are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. Kazama Studios does not receive any of this data.

2. What the app connects to

IPTVKZ connects only:

  • a) to the addresses of the lists, guides, images and videos you add, to show and play that content. These servers belong to third parties chosen by you and have their own policies;
  • b) to public connectivity check services (from Google and Microsoft), to show whether there is internet access, without sending any data from your account or your lists;
  • c) to YouTube, only when you ask for a trailer; in that case YouTube's rules apply;
  • d) in the LG TV version and the Android APK installed outside the stores, to GitHub, to check for and download signed updates. The LG Content Store and Google Play versions are updated only by the stores themselves, and the desktop version does not look for updates on its own.

3. On your home network

To add a list from your phone, the TV shows a QR Code with a single-use code, valid for 15 minutes. The data goes from the phone straight to the TV over your network, without passing through servers of Kazama Studios or third parties. The file created by “Baixar lista” (Download list) contains the list's username and password and stays with you: keep it safe and do not share it.

4. What we don't do

The app does not collect personal data, and it uses no ads, usage analytics, automatic error reports or advertising identifiers. We do not sell data. We receive nothing from the app; from this website, only visit measurement and what you choose to send through the support form (item 7).

5. How to delete your data

Delete the list in Configurações (Settings), in the app itself, or uninstall IPTVKZ: everything it stored is removed from the device. The app does not send us data, so we hold no copy to access, correct or delete, including for the purposes of the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD). To remove a report sent through the website form, write to contato@kazamastudios.com.br saying which report should be removed.

6. Age

IPTVKZ has no content of its own and is rated for all ages. What appears in it depends only on the lists each person adds, which may contain content unsuitable for minors. That is what the parental control (PIN) is for: it hides categories marked as adult. It does not replace supervision by a responsible adult.

7. On this website

  • This website uses Google Tag Manager, from Google, to measure visits in aggregate (how many people visit, which pages and where they come from). It may store cookies in your browser, and visit data is processed by Google under its own privacy policy. This applies only to the website: the app still sends no data. You can block these cookies in your browser settings. The website has no ads.
  • The Help button answers questions in your own browser, without sending what you type.
  • If you send the form on the support page, the report type, the part of the app, the moment, the device, the version and the message become a public issue on the project's GitHub. We try to remove links, usernames and passwords, but do not write that data. Your IP address is used for a few minutes only to limit abusive submissions, it is not stored or published, and the website runs on Cloudflare's infrastructure.
  • To show the latest version in the download area, your browser queries GitHub's public API. The download files are also served by GitHub; in that case GitHub's rules apply.

8. Third-party lists

The lists you add are provided by third parties, who may process your data (for example, username, password and IP address) under their own rules. Kazama Studios has no relationship with these providers and is not responsible for what they do with your data, or for piracy, fraud or scams related to those lists. See item 10 of the Terms of use.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling data differently, this page and the app's terms will be updated beforehand, and the app will ask you to accept them again.

10. Contact

Privacy questions and requests about your data: contato@kazamastudios.com.br. Help with the app: suporte@kazamastudios.com.br or the support page. Do not send links, usernames or passwords from your lists.
